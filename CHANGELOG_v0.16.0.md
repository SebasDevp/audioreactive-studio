# v0.16.1 · Arcane Living Tree

- Rebuilt Tree of Life from the supplied low-poly recursive-cylinder study.
- 3D perspective tree rendered to its own texture and composited inside the modular preset engine.
- Persistent tapered branch segments with real spatial direction and audio-driven growth.
- Bass drives reach/body, mids steer/curl, treble + spectral flux trigger forks and fine growth.
- Low energy retracts live growth along its own path; the permanent trunk and major limbs remain.
- No floor, arcane ring, loading label or scene text in the visual.
- Reactive palette lights, subtle luminous branch tips and restrained highlight roll-off.
- Tree render target runs at an adaptive cinematic resolution to keep performance high.
- Existing 24-world engine, feedback, output, transitions and controls preserved.
